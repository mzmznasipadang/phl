# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The site speaks to a broad, mostly Indonesian and regional maritime-industry audience, visiting in three languages (EN / ID / ZH):

- **Procurement and technical buyers** at energy, shipping, and industrial firms evaluating PHL as a shipbuilding or ship-repair vendor. They care about capability, capacity, track record, and compliance.
- **Government agencies and state-owned enterprises** (Pertamina, Pelindo, Bakamla, Kemenhub, KKP, Adaro, and similar) vetting credibility, certifications, and regulatory standing.
- **Partners, press, and prospective talent** forming a first impression of the company.

Context: most arrive while comparing yards or doing due diligence, often as non-native English readers (hence the EN / ID / ZH split). The job to be done is to decide whether PT. Pahala Harapan Lestari is credible, capable, and trustworthy enough to engage, then to make contact.

## Product Purpose

The official marketing website for PT. Pahala Harapan Lestari, a family-owned national shipyard in Pangkalpinang, Bangka Belitung, Indonesia, operating since 1982 with 100+ vessels delivered. The site communicates the company's heritage, its capabilities across shipbuilding (Vessels), ship repair (Repairs), and related services, its track record and standards (awards, ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 certifications), and converts that credibility into contact.

Success looks like a qualified visitor leaving with confidence in PHL: downloading the company profile and/or submitting the contact form to start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

**Established and grounded.** Steadfast, proven, dependable. The voice is confident but understated, factual, and free of hype: the quiet authority of a yard that has been delivering ships for four decades. Warmth comes from the family-shipyard craft story and pride in the work, not from playfulness.

Emotional goals: trust, reassurance, and respect. A visitor should feel they are looking at a serious, capable institution that will still be here in twenty years.

## Anti-references

This should NOT look or feel like:

- **Generic corporate brochureware.** Stock business photography, default SaaS section layouts, repeating icon-and-text cards, the interchangeable "AI made that" template.
- **A flashy consumer-tech startup.** Trendy gradients, playful illustration, oversized rounded everything, hype copy. Too informal for an industrial heritage firm.
- **A cold, purely industrial spec sheet.** Bare engineering-spec utilitarianism with no warmth, no human story, no pride of craft.
- **Cluttered and busy.** Dense walls of text, competing elements, no breathing room. Hard to scan and therefore hard to trust.

## Design Principles

1. **Proof over adjectives.** Trust is earned with evidence, not claims. Lead with the real vessels, the client logos, the awards, the ISO certifications, the 40+ year history. Show it; do not assert it.
2. **Steady, not flashy.** Dependability is the aesthetic. Favor calm, durable, proven-feeling choices over trend-chasing. Restraint here is a signal of seriousness, not timidity.
3. **Warmth inside the rigor.** The engineering competence is the spine; the family-craft story and the people are what keep it from reading cold. Let both show.
4. **Calm and scannable.** One clear idea per view, generous breathing room, an obvious reading path. A mixed, often non-native-English audience should never feel lost. Legibility earns trust.
5. **Three languages, one standard.** EN, ID, and ZH are equal citizens. Every design decision must hold up, and lead the visitor to contact, in all three.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target **WCAG 2.1 AA**.

- Body text contrast at least 4.5:1; large text at least 3:1. Watch white text over the navy gradient and any text on photographic overlays.
- Full keyboard navigation with visible focus states on the navbar, language switcher, footer directory, and contact form.
- Meaningful, descriptive alt text on the heavily image-led Vessels, Repairs, and About galleries; alt text is part of the brand voice.
- Reduced-motion alternatives for the customer marquee, the navbar hide-on-scroll, and the landing overlay fade (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`).
- Favor generous, legible type sizes for an older, mixed B2B and government readership.
- Maintain parity of content and accessibility across the EN / ID / ZH versions.
