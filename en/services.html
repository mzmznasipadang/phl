Repair and docking in Pangkalpinang.

Alongside new construction, PT. Pahala Harapan Lestari repairs and docks vessels at its yard: tugs back from heavy towing work, oil and deck barges, navigation and defense vessels, and passenger ships. Repaired vessels return to service in ports and terminals throughout Indonesia.

Repairs follow the same quality, environmental and occupational health and safety management as our new builds, in line with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001.
