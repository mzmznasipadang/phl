Profile

Over 100 vessels, one family yard, since 1982.

PT. Pahala Harapan Lestari is a family shipyard in Pangkalpinang, Bangka Belitung, building and repairing ships since 1982. More than 100 vessels later, we still build by standardization and serial production.

Our clients include Pertamina, Pelindo, Adaro and Sinarmas. Our quality, environmental and safety management follows ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and ISO 45001, and in 2021 we received the Paramakarya award from the President of the Republic of Indonesia.
